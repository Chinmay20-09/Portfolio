ENGINEER

A little about how I build

I’m Chinmay Kolte. I like taking a fuzzy problem, understanding its constraints, and turning it into a tool that feels clear and dependable.

Curiosity, made useful

The projects here reflect the questions I keep coming back to: how can AI agents coordinate without becoming opaque? How can an assistant remain useful when the network isn’t? How can infrastructure tell you what’s wrong before it becomes an incident?

I enjoy working across the stack, from stateful backends and local data to interfaces that make complex systems easier to understand. I care about practical tradeoffs, thoughtful details, and making the core idea work well.

My current work spans agent workflows, regional language and offline-first assistance, lightweight monitoring, and privacy-minded networking. I build to learn, then keep refining what proves useful.
